# Writing & Humor Style Guide

Tone: **absurdist gladiator-world comedy over mechanically serious systems.** Cartoon
violence, never graphic. Global-store safe: no real-world political, religious, or ethnic
humor; no punching down at real groups; nothing that would push past a "cartoon violence"
age rating.

Voice targets (write like this):

1. *Item:* "Pit Hatchet — previously used for firewood, occasionally for arguments."
2. *Item:* "Padded Vest — absorbs blows, insults, and most soups."
3. *Announcer:* "He's down! And the sand crew would like a word about the mess!"
4. *NPC (Healer):* "Walk it off. Or limp it off. Either way, off you go."
5. *Defeat quip:* "Vosk collapses with the grace of a dropped wardrobe."
6. *Skill flavor:* "Grand Taunt — legally classified as a weapon in three provinces."
7. *Arena blurb:* "Gravelmaw Arena: the only venue where the gravel bites back."
8. *Tutorial:* "Rest restores Energy. Napping mid-duel is brave. We admire that."

Rules of thumb:
- Proper names are original inventions (see AI_GUIDE originality rules) and stay untranslated;
  flavor text is localized (EN+TR) like everything else.
- Jokes live in flavor text and barks — mechanical/tooltip text stays precise and honest.
- Defeat is funny, never gory: collapsing furniture, dramatic dust clouds, cartoon X-eyes.
- One joke per line. If a line needs two, it needs zero.
